Our approach

We do not start from a slide deck. We start from a working reference platform — orchestration, governed AI agents, compliance-as-code, a real lakehouse, and a trained risk model — and adapt it to your portfolio, your core systems, and your regulatory frame. That collapses the usual 9–12 month "will it even work" risk into a 12-week stakeholder-grade delivery, then hardens to production from a known-good base.

This document lays out the platform as ten modules. For each: what is a must-do in the short term to stand up a credible, compliant, demonstrable platform — versus what we layer in long term for scale, automation, and enterprise breadth — plus the concrete features we bring to each.

The ten modules

01Orchestration CoreFoundation

The spine. Every delinquent customer is a long-running workflow over a real-time event bus — durable, replayable, and the single place every decision and action is sequenced.

▸ Short term Phase 1
  • Per-customer journey workflow with delinquency state machine MUST
  • Event bus + signal routing (channel event → workflow) MUST
  • Durable execution, retries, timers (PTP deadlines, validation windows)
  • Event store with full per-customer history (the audit trail)
▸ Long term Phase 2–3
  • Horizontal scale to millions of concurrent journeys
  • Workflow versioning & safe migration of in-flight cases
  • Exactly-once delivery guarantees, multi-region failover
  • Managed control plane (e.g. MSK + managed workflow service)
What we bring
Temporal workflowsKafka event spinePer-customer state machineSignal bridgeReplayable historyTimer-driven SLAs
02Strategy & DecisioningPolicy

The brain for "what should we do next, for whom, and through which channel" — expressed as versioned policy, not buried in code, so risk owns it and can change it safely.

▸ Short term Phase 1
  • Segmentation, treatment, and channel-routing as policy MUST
  • Every decision logged with full input context & version MUST
  • Hot-reload of policy changes (no redeploy)
  • Explainable decision trace ("why did we do that?")
▸ Long term Phase 2–3
  • Champion/challenger automation with auto-promotion gates
  • Business-user policy authoring UI with approval workflow
  • Treatment optimization loop driven by observed outcomes
  • Cross-product strategy library & reuse
What we bring
OPA / policy-as-codeSegmentation engineTreatment matrixChannel routingStrategy versioningDecision audit logChampion/challenger
03Compliance & ControlsRegulatory

Compliance enforced at the millisecond an action is taken — not reconstructed in a month-end batch. Citations live in the rules; every block is recorded, tamper-evident.

▸ Short term Phase 1
  • Reg F: call-frequency caps, time-of-day windows, validation notices MUST
  • Bankruptcy / SCRA / cease-and-desist suppression MUST
  • Pre-action compliance gate on every outbound MUST
  • Write-once, tamper-evident (WORM) audit at the data layer MUST
▸ Long term Phase 2–3
  • Full UDAAP / FCRA / state-law & internal-policy coverage
  • Regulatory change management workflow with versioned attestations
  • Automated examiner-ready reporting packs
  • Consent & preference management integration
